The threat

Without throttling, an attacker can guess thousands of passwords per minute until one works. 4-digit PINs have only 10,000 possibilities — trivial to brute-force online if no rate limit is in place. Common-PIN lists make it even faster: most users pick the same 20 or 30 PINs.

Your task

  1. The challenge has a login that accepts a 4-digit PIN with no rate limit.
  2. Try PINs from the common-PIN list until one works.
  3. The lesson completes the moment you find the correct PIN.

How this app blocks brute force

  • Flask-Limiter caps /login at 5 attempts per minute per IP.
  • Registration is throttled at 10/hour to prevent username spam.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt — even if a guess succeeded, the verification cost makes online brute force impractical.
