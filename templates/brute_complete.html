Missing Rate Limit

You brute-forced a 4-digit PIN by trying common values one after another. The endpoint had no rate limit, so the only friction was the size of the keyspace — and that's tiny for PINs.

Layered defenses against brute force

  • Rate limiting (this app: 5/min on /login).

    Caps how many guesses an attacker can make per minute — turns a seconds-long attack into a years-long one.

  • Slow password hashing (bcrypt cost 12).

    Each verification takes ~250 ms server-side. Combined with rate limiting, online brute force is impractical.

  • Strong password / PIN policy.

    Minimum length, complexity, and blocking common values shrinks the practical attack surface.

  • Account lockout / MFA.

    After repeated failures, lock the account or require a second factor — turning a brute-force attempt into a noisy, alertable event.
